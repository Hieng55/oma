# OMA Tattoo Reference-Fidelity Rework

Implementation decisions are derived from the client's explicit instruction to make the site more closely resemble `gangatattoolosangeles.com`, while retaining OMA content and the existing written constraints.

## 1. Vibe and references

- Client direction: closely resemble `gangatattoolosangeles.com`.
- Vibe: dark, bold, urban, image-led, confident.
- Primary visual reference: Ganga Tattoo Los Angeles website.
- Brand/content reference: `omatattoo.com` and supplied OMA assets.

## 2. Scroll journey

1. Full-screen OMA studio slideshow with the OMA wordmark.
2. Horizontal tattoo-style collection.
3. OMA artist directory.
4. Verified studio experience and client feedback.
5. Booking form.
6. Practical contact footer.

## 3. Energy curve

- Opening: high-impact and cinematic.
- Styles: exploratory and fast.
- Artists: dense, confident, and informative.
- Reviews/studio: calmer and trust-building.
- Booking: focused and decisive.

## 4. Feeling curve

1. **Recognition**: the real OMA studio fills the screen, with the brand mark presented at reference scale.
2. **Curiosity**: a lateral styles rail invites exploration through real OMA work.
3. **Confidence**: the artist grid presents the supplied team and specialties clearly.
4. **Trust**: verified studio facts and paraphrased client feedback slow the pace.
5. **Resolve**: a visually isolated booking panel gives one clear next action.

## 5. Peak

The visitor would say: “The OMA logo landed over a full-screen studio scene, then the tattoo work opened into a horizontal collection.”

The peak lives across the hero-to-styles handoff. The hero uses the largest visual scale; the style rail is the first interactive payoff.

## 6. Signature move

The hero brand mark acts as a depth mask: studio slides change behind it while the mark stays locked near the bottom, then compresses into the sticky navigation identity as the visitor leaves the hero.

## 7. Aesthetic range

- Family: premium-minimal with a dense urban/editorial artist directory.
- Palette: only the existing OMA design-system colors.
- Typography: existing approved families, with heavier weights and width treatment to approximate the reference's graphic impact without copying its proprietary typefaces.

## 8. World and assets

- Structure: distinct scenes, matching the reference website's section changes.
- World: natural documentary photography using supplied OMA studio, artist, and tattoo assets.
- No generated imagery is required.

## Grammar

**Gallery / catalog.** OMA's real offer is a portfolio and artist range. The reference itself behaves like a catalog: hero, style rail, artist directory, booking plate.

Why the other grammars lost:

- Filmic one-shot would overstate continuous motion that the reference does not use.
- Chaptered editorial is too text-heavy.
- Live surface does not fit a tattoo studio.
- Continuous world implies geographic travel.
- Typographic poster would underuse the supplied work.
- Split stage requires a comparison OMA does not have.
- Rhythmic cutlist would make booking and artist discovery too abrupt.

## Fingerprint gate

The registry was empty before this build. The plan therefore clears every existing row by default.

It's the site where the OMA mark stays locked over the studio while the scene changes, then opens into a wall of real tattoo work and artists.

## Authored silence

No empty scroll spans. The calmer review/studio scene is intentional contrast, not dead scroll.
